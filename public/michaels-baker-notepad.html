1.0 Brand Charter & Purity

Every cake is baked from scratch within 12 hours of delivery. 100% pure dairy butter, Belgian cocoa, Madagascar bourbon vanilla, and unbleached flour. No artificial preservatives or vegetable shortening.

2.0 Signature Cakes & Standard Pricing

Prices in PKR. Standard 2.5 lbs (8-10 servings):

  • Chocolate Fudge Cake: PKR 3,200 (Belgian ganache & Dutch cocoa sponge)
  • Vanilla Celebration Cake: PKR 2,900 (Madagascar vanilla & Swiss buttercream)
  • Red Velvet Cake: PKR 3,400 (Philadelphia cream cheese frosting)
  • Strawberry Cream Cake: PKR 3,100 (Fresh compote & vanilla chantilly)
  • Box of 6 Chocolate Cupcakes: PKR 1,800
  • Custom Birthday Cakes: From PKR 4,500

3.0 Fulfillment Policies

• Minimum 24 hours advance notice for signature cakes.
• 3 to 5 days required for multi-tiered wedding cakes.
• 50% confirmation deposit required via Bank Transfer, Raast, or Studio Handover.
• Temperature-controlled delivery across Karachi in insulated boxes.
